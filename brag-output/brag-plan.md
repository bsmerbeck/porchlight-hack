# Brag Plan: Porchlight

## What is this app?
Porchlight is an AI guardian for an aging parent's phone. An AI answers unknown calls and
scores them live for scam tactics (urgency, secrecy, odd payment like gift cards/crypto,
impersonation; risk 0-100). When a caller claims to be family, the REAL family member's
phone asks "Is this you calling?" and they tap No (or Yes + Face ID). A lamp beside the
phone (plus Hue bulbs) glows RED for a scam and GREEN for verified family, so the senior
never reads an app. The family dashboard keeps the transcript and risk reasons.
Built in 24 hours at the yconic hackathon, RI Startup Week. Live: https://porchlight-hack.web.app

## The angle
**The call Grandma almost took.** Not a feature list: one grandparent-scam call, start to
finish, with the lamp as the main character. The whole video is lit by the lamp's state color.

## Hook (0-4.6s)
Near-black, warm charcoal. Margaret's porch lantern sits amber on the left; a RINGING pill under it.
Unknown caller card with pulsing rings. A transcript bubble types out the cloned-voice plea:
"Grandma, it's Brenden… I've been arrested. I need bail money. Don't tell Mom."
Beat line: "A cloned voice. A panicked grandson. Grandma reaches for her purse."

## Key moments
- **Screening (4.6-9.6s).** Lamp and room go screening blue. "Porchlight AI answers first."
  Second caller line (gift cards, today, don't tell Mom). Scam risk counts 0 -> 95 while the
  bar sweeps blue -> amber -> red; four tactic chips pop: Urgency, Secrecy, Odd payment, Impersonation.
- **Verifying (9.6-14.2s).** Lamp back to amber, pulsing (the app's verifying state). The real
  Brenden's phone: "Is this you calling?" with "Yes, it's me · Face ID" / "No, block this call".
  He taps No.
- **Payoff (14.2-18.4s).** Room floods red, lamp red, **SCAM BLOCKED**. "The lamp turned red.
  Margaret hung up." Chips: Risk 95 · Transcript saved · 4 reasons · Family notified.
- **The flip (18.4-21.2s).** "And when it really is Brenden?" -> lamp and room green, **VERIFIED**.

## Outro (21.2-26.5s)
$4.9B count-up in amber: lost to fraud by Americans over 60 in 2024, up 43% (FBI IC3).
Then the Porchlight wordmark with the lamp, "A cloned voice can fool Mom. It can't fool her
family." and the porchlight-hack.web.app pill.

## Tone
Cinematic, quiet, warm. The color changes are the storytelling; type stays calm.
Music: the template's bed at low volume; SFX from the template's pack.

## Formats
