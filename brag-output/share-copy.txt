=== Variant A (LinkedIn) ===
"Grandma, it's Brenden. I've been arrested. I need bail money. Don't tell Mom."

That call can now be made with a cloned voice and 30 seconds of audio from someone's Instagram. Americans over 60 lost $4.9B to fraud in 2024, up 43% in a year (FBI IC3).

So in 24 hours at the yconic hackathon (RI Startup Week, Providence) I built Porchlight: an AI guardian for an aging parent's phone.

- An AI answers unknown calls and scores them live for scam tactics: urgency, secrecy, gift cards/crypto, impersonation.
- When a caller claims to be family, the REAL family member's phone asks "Is this you calling?" One tap: No, block it. Or Yes + Face ID.
- A lamp next to Mom's phone glows red for a scam, green for verified family. She never has to open an app.

A cloned voice can fool Mom. It can't fool her family.
